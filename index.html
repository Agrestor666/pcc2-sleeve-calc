■ Mill tolerance (trequired)

Defaults per Totalenergies wall-thickness sheet: 12.5% for sleeve D < 457.2 mm (NPS < 18"), 8.0% for D ≥ 457.2 mm. Formula: trequired = tcalculated / (1 − f).

◊ Weld Joint Efficiency
