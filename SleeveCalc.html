▲ Process Data

When P ≥ 690 bar, allowable stress S is taken from Table K-1 (B31.3-2024, SI) for the selected material at design temperature Tmax (table in °C, stress in MPa; converted to bar for Eq. (34a): 1 MPa = 10 bar).

When P < 690 bar, allowable stress S is taken from Table A-1 (B31.3-2024, SI) for the selected material at design temperature Tmax (table in °C, stress in MPa; converted to bar for Chapter II: 1 MPa = 10 bar).

Default 0.4 matches ASME B31.3 Table 304.1.1 for ferritic steels at ≤ 482 °C (≤ 900 °F). At higher temperatures, Y increases—edit per your edition.

t = P · D 2 · (S · E + P · Y) + CA
CA – corrosion allowance [mm]

S – allowable stress of sleeve material
E – weld joint efficiency (0.80 or 1.00)
Y – temperature coefficient (ASME B31.3 Table 304.1.1). Page default 0.4 is the usual value for ferritic steels at T ≤ 482 °C (≤ 900 °F), with other conditions per the code; change if your case differs.
